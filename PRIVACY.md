# Privacy

This repository holds configuration and instructions only. It runs no code on your machine and collects nothing by itself.

When you use the BDK Native tools, your assistant talks to BDK's server at mcp.thebdk.com on your behalf, after you sign in to your BDK account in your browser. How BDK handles your data is described in BDK's privacy policy:

https://thebdk.com/privacy
